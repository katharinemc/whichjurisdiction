FAQ

How is my result calculated?

Every answer carries fixed point values across the jurisdictions, summed and converted to a percentage. If your top result clears a majority on its own, that's what you get — one card. If it doesn't, the quiz keeps adding the next-highest result until the cards on screen add up to a majority, so a landslide is one card and a genuinely split verdict can take two or three.

Why isn't my jurisdiction on here?

The author didn't have a stereotype for it sharp and affectionate enough to earn a spot next to the others. Depending on how you think about it, this is a good thing.

Is this making fun of Orthodoxy?

It's making fun of Orthodox people, lovingly, which is a different thing. If you can't tell the difference, this quiz will not convince you, and you should probably close the tab.

Do you track anything?

The quiz keeps a running count of how the results break down, so I can see whether the scoring is lopsided. No names, no accounts, nothing tied to you individually.

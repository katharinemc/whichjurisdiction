FAQ

Is this serious?

No. It's a satirical personality quiz. If you recognize the jokes, they're for you; if you don't, no harm done.

How is the result calculated?

Every answer is worth a fixed number of points toward each jurisdiction. Your nineteen answers are summed and converted to a percentage. Nothing is random, and nothing is stored on a server — the scoring happens entirely in your browser.

Why can't I find every jurisdiction in the directory?

The directory only lists a few. The rest, you'll have to take the quiz to meet.
